## How it works

### Heading

**A structured approach from requirement to delivery**

1. **Understand the requirement** – Discuss project scope, skills, data types, volumes, instructions, timelines and acceptance criteria.
2. **Plan the workflow** – Confirm resources, onboarding, task process, quality checks and reporting.
3. **Onboard and coordinate** – Brief contributors and coordinate access, task allocation and project communication.
4. **Execute and review** – Track progress, review work against agreed guidelines and coordinate corrections.
5. **Report and reconcile** – Share agreed progress and delivery reports and reconcile completed and accepted work.

Do not promise client approval, turnaround times or acceptance rates that are outside Step-Up Career's control.
